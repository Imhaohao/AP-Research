01 · Mechanism

Two modes of use — only one protects learning

Substitutive

AI replaces thinking

  • ✕ 42% of help requests with zero attempt first
  • ✕ ↓ understanding even when output volume ↑
  • ✕ EEG: weakest coupling vs. solo writing
  • ✕ One substitutive episode hurts later performance
Complementary

AI scaffolds thinking

  • ✓ Explain-first prompts → ↑ understanding
  • ✓ Quasi-exp.: comprehension & CT gains (n = 110)
  • ✓ Mediated by prompt craft
  • ✓ Effect rides on how input is framed
Lehmann et al., 2025 · Kosmyna et al., 2024 · Eltahir & Babiker, 2024

Center stage; gesture left then right. Three labs converge: substitution collapses learning; complementarity grows it. The dial between them is prompting. Pause before advancing.

03 · Design

Two-arm quasi-experiment · ≤ 60 min · same platform

Volunteers → sequence-based assignment · treatment arms collapsed for primary analysis

1 Pre-baseline (~5′)
2 Module / placebo (~25–30′)
3 CRAFT writing (~15–20′)
4 Post-survey (~5′)
5 Optional 1-mo ping
Lehmann et al., 2025 · Eltahir & Babiker, 2024

Dense slide — speak slowly; point at each step. Only the middle 25 minutes differ between arms; everything else is shared. That isolates the instructional manipulation.

03 · Sampling

PAUSD high schools · volunteer recruitment

  • Gunn & Palo Alto High · Schoology + posters + form
  • Identical incentive across arms (e.g., GPT Pro voucher)
  • Exclude >1 hr prior PE bootcamps
Target n

30

Powers two-tailed t-test, large d (~0.95), α = .05, β = .20 — conservative for a 25′ dose.

Acknowledge n = 30 up front for Reflect points. Small but pre-registered; large expected effect; every flagged prompt can be hand-audited. Honest about volunteer bias.

05 · Limits

What this study cannot claim yet

  • Single district · volunteer sample
  • Underpowered 3-arm split
  • Auto-grader weakest on Request Elaboration
  • Optional retention → attrition risk
  • CRAFT lab task ≠ full coursework transfer
Next
  • n ≈ 90 · multi-district
  • Restore prompt-bank contrast
  • 3-mo coursework follow-up
